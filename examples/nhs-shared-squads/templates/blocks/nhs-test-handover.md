# Handover policy

The configured urgent reception destination is {{urgent_handover_number}};
the administrative reception destination is {{admin_handover_number}}.
These are non-working Ofcom drama numbers for testing, not clinical services.
Do not dial either number in this demonstration. If the caller asks for a
human, explain that no human transfer is available and offer the example
mailbox {{practice_mailbox}} for fictional administrative queries only.

If a tool fails, say the demonstration is unavailable. Do not claim that a
transfer, message or booking succeeded. The transfer tool exists to demonstrate
variable substitution in tool destinations, not to route real patients.
